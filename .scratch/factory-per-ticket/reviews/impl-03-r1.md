## The deviation

The reordering in [COORDINATOR.md:9-10](/Users/sanphan/.t3/worktrees/skills/refactor-factory-per-ti-b6754f35c6988ade/plugins/software/skills/factory/COORDINATOR.md#L9-L10) (**another session's `thread` → settle; PR merged → Close, merged; closed/wontfix → clean up and settle; otherwise not claimed → settle**) **resolves the plan 1.6 conflict correctly**:

- Because `res unclaim <n>` ([resources.mjs:285-293](/Users/sanphan/.t3/worktrees/skills/refactor-factory-per-ti-b6754f35c6988ade/plugins/software/skills/factory/scripts/resources.mjs#L285-L293)) sets `claimed: false` while preserving `rec.thread`, a coordinator woken by dispatcher step 3 after `unclaim` passes step 1 and executes the closed/`wontfix` cleanup (stopping workers, clearing `flag-human`, `unwatch-pr`, settling) instead of bailing early on `claimed: false`.
- If a ticket was closed, unclaimed, reopened, and re-claimed by a *new* coordinator before the old one wakes, step 1 still catches `rec.thread !== this session` first and prevents the old coordinator from touching the new owner's state.
- If a stray turn wakes a coordinator after `Close, merged` already unclaimed it, step 2 re-enters idempotent `Close, merged` safely; if unclaimed for any other reason (`closed_unmerged`), the final clause settles it.

One small improvement to step 2 ([COORDINATOR.md:10](/Users/sanphan/.t3/worktrees/skills/refactor-factory-per-ti-b6754f35c6988ade/plugins/software/skills/factory/COORDINATOR.md#L10)): call `res unclaim <n>` rather than `res reclaim <n>` for closed/`wontfix` tickets. When the dispatcher already ran `unclaim`, calling `unclaim` again is an idempotent no-op; when the coordinator itself notices closed/`wontfix` first (e.g., on a worker turn before the next dispatcher run), `unclaim` immediately frees `Max tickets` and `waitingOn` instead of leaving `claimed: true` until the next dispatch.
